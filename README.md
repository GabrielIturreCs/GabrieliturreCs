## Building KILO

I'm the founder of **KILO**, a mobile-first commerce platform designed to let a business start selling from day one and scale without changing its operating system.

KILO turns the phone a merchant already has into the core of the business:

- **POS + barcode scanner** using the phone camera
- **Inventory, cash management and customers** in one workflow
- **Digital payments** through QR and payment-provider integrations
- **Payment terminal integrations** when the business needs dedicated hardware
- **Digital receipts via QR / NFC** instead of depending on a printer
- **Online catalog synchronized with physical sales and stock**
- **Multi-register, warehouse and multi-branch capabilities** as the operation grows
- **AI-assisted workflows** to reduce repetitive operational work

The product philosophy is simple: **no PC, scanner, printer or payment terminal should be mandatory to make the first sale.** Start with a phone; add infrastructure only when growth requires it.
